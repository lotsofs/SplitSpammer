# Changelog

What changed for users of the SplitSpammer component in each release. There are no releases yet.

## Unreleased

### Added

- The settings show a tree of splits that you can collapse and expand, with a checkbox for each. A split only counts when it and the groups above it are checked. Right-click a group to expand or collapse the tree, or to check, uncheck or reset a branch. Your choices are saved in the layout.
- The component's settings show whether GTA San Andreas was found, and say if it is not version 1.0. If the game runs as administrator, LiveSplit must too.
